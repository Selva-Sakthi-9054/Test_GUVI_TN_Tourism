When to go

A year of festivals

Dates follow the Tamil lunar calendar and shift each year, so confirm locally before booking.

January

Pongal

The harvest festival: sweet rice cooked in the open, decorated cattle and kolam patterns at every doorstep.

Jan – Feb

Thaipusam

Devotees honour Lord Murugan with processions and kavadi offerings at temples across the state.

April – May

Chithirai Festival

Madurai celebrates the celestial wedding of Meenakshi and Sundareswarar with grand chariot processions.

Sep – Oct

Navaratri & Golu

Homes display tiers of dolls, and neighbours visit each other for songs, snacks and stories.

Nov – Dec

Karthigai Deepam

Rows of oil lamps light homes, and a huge beacon is lit on the hill at Tiruvannamalai.

Dec – Jan

Margazhi Season

Chennai fills with Carnatic music and Bharatanatyam performances in a month-long cultural calendar.

Before you go

Practical tips

General notes only. Check current conditions and official sources for permits, timings and fees.

Best season

November to February is the most comfortable for most of the plains. Hill stations are lovely from March to June.

Temple etiquette

Dress modestly, remove footwear before entering, and check whether photography is allowed inside.

Getting around

Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore and Tiruchirappalli have airports. Trains and state buses link most major towns.

Language

Tamil is the main language. English is widely understood in cities and tourist areas, and a few Tamil greetings go a long way.
